TG OS

Fun + Utility Pack

A playable mini desktop with games, productivity tools, and a full settings menu.

How to Open

  1. Open this folder in a terminal.
  2. Run python3 -m http.server 8000.
  3. Visit http://localhost:8000 in your browser.

Then click any Launch button below to open an app window.
